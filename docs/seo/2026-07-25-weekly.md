## The constraint

**Still indexing, not content or CTR.** The site has zero Search Console
impressions and zero clicks across 90 days, and the two blog URLs are
discovered-but-unindexed rather than indexed. Per the skill's own staging
(not indexed → indexed but no rankings → impressions but no clicks →
clicks but no engagement → growth), we're at the first stage. There is
nothing yet to optimise for title/meta CTR or content-market-fit — GSC
`queries` and `pages` both returned empty results for the full 28-day and
90-day windows, so there's no query data to act on this week.

This is expected at this stage (a domain-verified property days old, one
post live), not a red flag yet. It becomes a real problem if `/blog/` and
the post are still "Discovered — not indexed" after roughly a week from
sitemap submission (25 Jul) — that's the trigger to actually investigate
rather than just wait.
